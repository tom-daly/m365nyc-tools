## Key Trends

**Growing buying influence** — Total buying influence has risen steadily: 80% (2023) → 86% (2024) → 88% (2025)

**Fewer passive attendees** — "No Direct Input" has dropped from 19% to 11%, meaning the audience is increasingly composed of people who influence or make purchasing decisions.

**C-Suite representation growing** — Executive-level attendees have grown from 21% (2023) → 23% (2024) → 28% (2025), approaching nearly 1 in 3.

**Developer engagement rebounding** — Developer job roles: 13% → 10% → 13%. As a self-identified user type, Developers went from 21% to 25% (2024→2025) while most other user types declined.

**More specialized audience** — General IT Pro/Admin as a user type has shifted from 40% to 28%, reflecting a move toward more specialized, technical decision-makers rather than generalist IT staff.

**Consistent reach** — ~290–320 attendees per year with 200+ unique organizations each year demonstrates reliable, sustained audience reach.
